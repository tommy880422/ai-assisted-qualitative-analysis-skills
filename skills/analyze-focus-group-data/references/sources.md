## Primary Focus-Group Analysis Sources

`Barbour-2018-Doing focus groups.pdf`

- Chapters 8-9: coding-frame development, comparison, absences, complexity, group interaction, discourse-informed interpretation, recommendations, and writing.

`Krueger-2015-Focus groups - A practical guide for applied research.pdf`

- Chapter 6: systematic, verifiable, sequential, and continuous analysis; data forms; debriefing; coding; classic and computer-assisted strategies.
- Chapter 7: reporting for different audiences and purposes.

`Stewart-2015-Focus groups - Theory and practice.pdf`

- Chapter 7: epistemological orientation, disciplinary focus, order, time, intensity, expression, individuals versus groups, content analysis, quantitative summaries, and software.

## Advanced and Culturally Responsive Sources

`Fern-2001-Advanced focus group research.pdf`

- Chapters 4 and 6: qualitative and quantitative summaries, moderator bias, independence, degrees of freedom, unit of analysis, interpretation, and generalizability.

`Hall-2020-Focus groups.pdf`

- Chapters 4 and 8: multicultural validity, inquirer reflexivity, transcription, nonverbal communication, computer assistance, and culturally responsive interpretation.

## Software Source

`Mortelmans-2025-Doing qualitative data analysis with NVivo.pdf`

- Chapter 15: focus-group transcription, comparison with interviews, classifications, and queries.

Preserve differences among the sources. A practical summary approach, interaction-focused interpretation, culturally responsive analysis, and advanced methodological critique answer different analytic needs.
