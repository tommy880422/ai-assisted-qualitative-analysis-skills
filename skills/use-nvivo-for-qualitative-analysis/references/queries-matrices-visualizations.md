Do not begin with the query menu. Begin with the analytic question.

## Common Query Uses

- **Coding query:** retrieve intersections, unions, proximity, or exclusions among codes and cases.
- **Text search:** locate a term and its variants, then read complete context; do not equate vocabulary with concept.
- **Matrix coding query:** compare codes by cases, groups, attributes, sources, or time periods.
- **Crosstab:** inspect coded patterns across classifications or attributes.
- **Word frequency:** explore vocabulary and data quality; not a theme detector.
- **Compound query:** sequence or combine search and coding conditions.
- **Coding comparison:** examine team coding only when the project's methodological model calls for it.
- **Group query:** locate project items related by metadata or analytic links.

## Interpret Matrix Results

1. Check whether cells contain sources, cases, references, coverage, or another measure.
2. Verify denominator and aggregation behavior.
3. Open cell content and read context.
4. Compare substantive meaning, not cell magnitude alone.
5. Record exceptions and missing data.
6. Export a human-readable evidence matrix with traceable locations.

## Visualizations

Use mind maps before or during conceptual planning, concept maps for explicit ideas and relations, project maps to inspect NVivo item connections, framework matrices for case-by-theme summaries, and charts or hierarchy views for descriptive exploration.

Treat every visualization as a provisional analytic object. Define what nodes, sizes, colors, distances, and links mean before interpreting them.
